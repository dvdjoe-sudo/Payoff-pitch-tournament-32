# Logo credits

Each club on the team page and the bracket uses a real cap logo. None of these marks was drawn for this site. To swap a file, change `logo` in [data/teams.json](data/teams.json).

## Already in the repo

These cap marks were already stored in `assets/logos/`. They are the 1986 sheet for the franchise, kept where that mark is the club named in the field. Los Angeles is the interlocking LA. Montreal is the Expos M. Oakland is the green A's. The Angels file is the halo A.

ATL, BAL, BOS, CHC, CIN, CWS, DET, HOU, KC, LAA, LAD, MIL, MIN, MON, NYM, NYY, OAK, PHI, PIT, SD, SEA, SF, STL, TEX, TOR.
